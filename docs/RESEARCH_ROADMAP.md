# Research Roadmap

## Current baseline

The repository currently provides a transparent TF-IDF + Logistic Regression sentiment baseline for Hindi-English code-mixed text.

## Stage 1 — Linguistic preprocessing

- Token-level language identification
- Transliteration normalization
- Informal spelling normalization
- Emoji and social-media normalization
- Robust handling of mixed Devanagari and Latin scripts

## Stage 2 — Representation learning

- Multilingual Transformer baselines
- Code-mixed language representations
- Domain-adaptive pretraining experiments
- Contrastive representation learning

## Stage 3 — Robust evaluation

- Cross-domain evaluation
- Class imbalance analysis
- Robustness to spelling and transliteration variation
- Error analysis by language/script pattern
- Statistical comparison of models

## Stage 4 — Explainability and trustworthiness

- Token-level attribution
- Confidence calibration
- Perturbation-based robustness tests
- Bias and subgroup analysis where appropriate
- Reproducible experiment tracking

## Stage 5 — Multimodal and generative research

- Code-mixed multimodal sentiment and emotion analysis
- LLM-based classification and explanation
- Retrieval-augmented analysis
- Human evaluation of generated explanations

## Long-term research direction

The broader research portfolio connects language intelligence with trustworthy AI, multimodal learning, agentic systems, and quantum-enhanced optimization. Quantum research is maintained as a separate direction so that each repository can remain focused and reproducible.
